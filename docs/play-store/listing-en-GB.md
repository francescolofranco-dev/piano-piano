# Piano Piano

## Short description

Learn beginner piano step by step with live mic pitch and rhythm feedback.

## Full description

Practice piano slowly, step by step — just like the name says.

Piano Piano listens to your acoustic piano or keyboard through your device microphone and tells you immediately whether you hit the right key, struck right on the beat, and held each note for its full duration.

LIVE FEEDBACK ON EVERY NOTE
Watch upcoming notes scroll along a clear treble staff with proportional duration bars. As you play, Piano Piano evaluates three things in real time:
• Key accuracy ("Wrong: F4" vs. green match)
• Onset timing ("Perfect!", "Too soon!", "Too late!")
• Hold duration ("Too short!", "Too long!", or full green hold)

STEP-BY-STEP BEGINNER EXERCISES
Start with five focused starter drills covering rhythm pulses, quarter/half/whole note holds, five-finger C position, broken C major triads, and Ode to Joy. Adjust the tempo (50–120 BPM) and listen to any exercise first with the built-in piano preview.

NO PIANO NEARBY? USE TOUCH MODE
Switch to Touch mode at any time to play and test exercises directly on the responsive 2-octave on-screen piano with built-in low-latency sound.

PRIVATE & OFFLINE
All microphone audio is analysed locally on your device in real time and never recorded, saved, or uploaded. No account, no ads, and no internet connection required.
